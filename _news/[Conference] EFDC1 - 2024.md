With this edition, the European Turbulence Conference (ETC) and European Fluid Mechanics Conference (EFMC) were merged, giving rise to the First European Fluid Dynamics Conference (EFDC1), held in Aachen (DE). There, I presented my work on the melting dynamics of floating ice cylinders.

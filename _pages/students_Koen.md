In Koen's Master thesis project we exploited the analogy of a Taylor--Couette setup as an ''infinite channel'' to study how turbulent shear affects the surface morphology of melting ice. We placed a cylinder of ice in a quiescent circular water tank, and had the ice rotate. Turbulence in the tank enhanced the heat transfer away from the ice, hence increasing the melt rate, and vertical and radial shear at the surface generated fluid instabilities which eroded the ice forming patterns.
